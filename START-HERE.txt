FAN GUYS OFFICE — V3.1

Your app is already built. You do NOT need to run a build.

1. Unzip Fan-Guys-Office-V3.1.zip.
2. Replace your repository files, including the ENTIRE old /docs folder.
3. Keep GitHub Pages set to: Deploy from a branch → main → /docs.
4. Overwrite the old .github/workflows/pages.yml with the disabled copy included here.
   If hidden folders cannot upload from your phone, delete the old Pages workflow.
5. Open your existing website. Accept Save & reload if offered, then reopen old tabs.

KEEP THE SAME WEBSITE ADDRESS. DO NOT CLEAR SAFARI WEBSITE DATA.
That data contains your saved documents and signatures.

WHAT’S INCLUDED
- Red Fan Guys Office branding and home-screen icons.
- Spreadsheets: Excel/CSV, formulas, tabs and a phone-friendly row editor.
- Writer: 13 blue-logo Fan Guys templates, basic Word import, Word/PDF export.
- Jobs & contacts, linked office files, notes and checklists.
- Complete backup and restore for PDFs AND the new office files.
- Existing PDF tools and GFY form fixes retained.

NEW: MORE TEMPLATES AND CARDS & FLYERS
- Writer now has 31 templates in 6 groups: Letters, Job forms, Service,
  Administration, Business forms, Safety, and Contracts & legal.
  Business forms: invoice, estimate, receipt, past-due reminder, W-9 and
  insurance request, customer/vendor info, time sheet, travel and mileage log.
  Safety: job hazard analysis, aerial lift pre-use inspection, toolbox talk,
  incident report, lockout/tagout log.
  Contracts & legal: subcontractor, independent contractor, mutual NDA,
  annual maintenance agreement, installation warranty. These are starting
  points, not legal advice. Have an attorney review them before relying on them.
- Cards & flyers (home screen and menu): business cards in 3 styles and
  flyers with an optional photo and tear-off phone tabs. Exports a 10-card
  Letter sheet (fits 10-per-sheet 2 x 3.5 in card stock, print at 100%),
  a print-shop card file with bleed, and a Letter flyer PDF.

FAN GUYS TEMPLATES
Open Writer. Choose a template or tap Show all 13 templates. Pick a saved
job first if you want the app to fill in its available contact and site details.
Edit the bracketed placeholders. The blue logo is included in Word and PDF exports.
To change the logo layout or save your contact details for future documents,
open Blue logo and letterhead details. The ZIP also includes 13 Word copies
in Ready-to-use-Word-templates.

OFFICE FILES
Use Save now before leaving important work. For export, wait until the file is
ready, then tap Share / Save to Files. Nothing is sent to a document server.

BACK UP
Use Backup & restore → Prepare complete backup. Store that ZIP privately.
Backups contain documents and signature images and are not encrypted.
Restore adds missing records and keeps current versions with matching IDs.
Reload after restoring. Clearing browser data can erase files.

LIMITS
This is the first expanded office release, not full Microsoft Office compatibility.
Word/Excel imports make simplified editable copies. Keep and download originals.
Advanced Excel features, exact Word layout and presentations are not included.
White-out is a visual cover, not secure redaction. Lock fields is not a password.
Open optional tools while online before using them offline.

README.md explains supported formats and formulas.
TEST-RESULTS.md lists actual tests and what still needs a physical iPhone check.
